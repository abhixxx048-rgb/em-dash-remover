## How do I remove em dashes in Chrome?

You have three options, fastest to most thorough:

1. **Find and replace.** In a text box, there's no built-in find-and-replace, so this only works in editors like Google Docs (Ctrl+H, paste an em dash into "Find"). Tedious and per-document.
2. **The web tool.** Paste your text into the [Em Dash Remover](/) on this site, copy the cleaned result back out. Best for long passages where you also want the AI-tell score.
3. **A browser extension.** Install once, and em dashes get handled in place - no copy, no paste, no tab-switching. This is the right tool when you're typing across many sites.

If you only clean text occasionally, the web tool is enough. If you do it daily, the extension pays for itself in saved clicks.

## How to automatically remove em dashes when typing

The [em dash remover Chrome extension](/tools/browser-extension) watches the field you're typing in and rebalances em dashes the moment they appear - no button to press.
